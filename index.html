our story

About BL Blossom & Co.

BL Blossom & Co. began with a simple idea: gifting should feel personal, not overwhelming. Based in Las Vegas, Nevada, we design custom gift baskets for the moments that matter — birthdays, weddings, graduations, bridal celebrations, baby showers, anniversaries, holidays, romantic gifts, and the quiet thank-yous in between.

Every basket starts as a conversation. We ask about the person you're celebrating — their favorite colors, their go-to brands, the little things that make them feel truly seen — and build something around them, at a budget that works for you.

We believe luxury doesn't have to mean expensive, and thoughtful doesn't have to mean plain. Just beautiful, considered gifts for the people you love — hand-arranged, one basket at a time.

getting it to you

Delivery & Pickup

BL Blossom & Co. proudly serves Las Vegas and the surrounding areas. Here's how pickup and delivery work.

Service Area

We deliver throughout Las Vegas and the surrounding valley. Let us know your location when you order and we'll confirm coverage.

Delivery Pricing

Delivery pricing is based on distance from our studio and is calculated once we know your delivery location.

Hotel & Strip Delivery

Delivering to a Las Vegas Strip hotel or resort? These deliveries may include an additional fee to cover check-in, security, and concierge drop-off.

Local Pickup

Prefer to pick up your order yourself? Local pickup is available — our studio location is shared once your order is confirmed.

Timing: exact delivery windows and final pricing are confirmed at the time of booking, based on your requested date and location. We recommend booking 1–2 weeks ahead when possible.

good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order?
We recommend placing your order at least 1–2 weeks before your date, especially during busy seasons like holidays and wedding season. Rush orders may be accommodated depending on availability — just ask.
Do you require a deposit?
Yes. Custom orders require a 50% deposit before we purchase any products on your behalf. The remaining balance is due once your basket is complete.
Can I choose the items inside?
Absolutely. Every basket is built around your input — colors, interests, favorite brands, and budget all shape what goes inside.
Are baskets exactly the same as photos?
Baskets are inspired by the style and quality shown in photos, but exact items may vary based on availability. We always maintain the same look, feel, and value.
Do you offer fresh flowers?
Yes — you can choose fresh or artificial flowers for your basket, depending on your preference and occasion.
Can you create gifts for men?
Definitely. We design baskets for everyone — just let us know who you're celebrating and their interests.
Do you deliver to Las Vegas hotels?
Yes, we deliver to hotels and resorts on and off the Strip. Hotel/Strip deliveries may include an additional fee.
Can I request luxury brands?
Yes! Designer and premium brand items can be added to any basket. These add-ons may increase your final price.
What happens if a product is unavailable?
Because every basket is individually created, occasional substitutions may be necessary. We always substitute with something of equal style, quality, and value.

Every BL Blossom & Co. basket is individually created by hand, which means small substitutions may occasionally happen — always with the same style, quality, and value you expect.
